First chat after a quiet spell

So a message after a lull is harder to miss. Only that first message is marked; the ones after it are normal until chat goes quiet again. Your own messages restart the count. Vibration works in Chrome on Android; Firefox and iPhones don't let websites vibrate, so use the sound there. It only works while the chat page is on screen.

Connection problems

Short drops reconnect quietly (a yellow banner shows the tries). If a platform still can't reconnect after 3 tries, the banner turns red and the first sound plays. UniChat tries 6 more times; if those fail too, it stops, the second sound plays and that platform's status pill turns red until you press Reconnect on the chat page.

Phones pause web pages in the background (another app, screen off), which drops the connections. UniChat doesn't count those as problems: when you come back it reconnects straight away and quietly, Twitch and TikTok first, then the others, and fills in the chat you missed where the platform allows it: Twitch (see above), TikTok and Kick.

Display & layout

How chat looks on the chat page and overlay.

Stream preview

Preview opens your stream in a popup on the chat page, using Beam's player (beamstream.gg). The player only loads while the popup is open and the page is on screen, so it uses no data otherwise. TikTok, Kick and the others can't be shown this way (TikTok doesn't allow its LIVE player inside other sites). On the narrowest phones the About button steps aside to make room.

Automatic puts the Alerts panel to the right of the chat, and above it on narrow or portrait screens (phones, vertical monitors). It starts at 20% of the space. On the chat page, drag the line between the chat and the panel to resize it; double-click it to reset.

Ticking ✓ on an alert or question fades it out of the panel after the time above (it stays in the chat). Click the tick again before then to undo.

While you were away: the chat page keeps its last messages for 2 hours, so a reload or reopening the app brings them back. Coming back after switching apps, reloading or reopening, what was sent meanwhile is filled in where the platform allows it (Twitch, TikTok and Kick; Velora, Blaze, Nimo TV and X can't), in time order under a While you were away line and highlighted for the time above, without sounds or popups. Alerts you missed (gifts, subs, follows…) go to the Alerts panel only, not into the chat.

7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ: their global emotes plus the ones set up for your Twitch channel (above), turned into pictures when anyone on any platform types them. The emote lists come from 7tv.io and api.betterttv.net (FrankerFaceZ through BetterTTV), with no login.

Twitch GIFs: Tier 2 and 3 subscribers can post GIFs from GIPHY in Twitch chat. They load from GIPHY's servers (media.giphy.com). Turn them off to show each GIF's caption instead, with nothing loaded from GIPHY.

TikTok, Velora, Blaze and Nimo TV send profile pictures with chat. Twitch and Kick don't, so UniChat looks them up by username (Twitch with IVR, a free public Twitch API; Kick on kick.com) and remembers them in this browser for a week. Turn profile pictures off to stop these lookups.

Twitch

Read anonymously, no login needed.

Shows chat, bits, Hype Chats, subs, gift subs, raids and announcements. Follows and channel points need a Twitch login, so they're not included.

Twitch doesn't resend chat you missed (for example while your phone had UniChat in the background). After reconnecting, UniChat fetches the channel's recent chat from recent-messages.robotty.de, a free service that Chatterino and other chat apps use, and adds what you missed, without sounds or popups. Turn this off to never contact it.

TikTok

Uses Euler Stream's free tier (free forever, no card).

No key needed: is read through UniChat's relay, which keeps the Euler key on Cloudflare. If the relay is ever down, switch on your own key below.

A backup key is saved in this browser. It's only used while the switch above is on.

  1. Create a free account at eulerstream.com.
  2. Copy your API key from their dashboard.
  3. Paste it above and press Save. It stays in this browser.

Quiet lines show in the chat without a sound, popup or alert. Joins show once per viewer every 10 minutes and fade out of the chat after the time above. Gift combos alert once, when the combo finishes.

Kick

Read from Kick's public chat feed, no login needed.

Shows chat, subs, gifted subs and KICKs, plus follows when Kick includes the follower's name.

Advanced: chatroom ID

UniChat finds your chatroom on kick.com by itself. Only if Kick blocks that (the status says so), open kick.com/api/v2/channels/yourchannel in your browser, find "chatroom":{"id": and enter the number after it here. Leave it at 0 otherwise.

Velora

Read as a guest, no login needed.

Shows chat, replies, raids and channel-point redemptions. Follows, subs and Volts are only sent to apps you log in to, so they're not included, and emotes show as their :names:.

Blaze

Read as a guest, no login needed.

Shows chat with emotes, tips, subs and gifted subs. Raids you send show as a note in chat.

Resonite

Your chat and alerts on an in-game panel. Turn this on on the PC you play on.

Download the UniChat panel

For Resonite: drag the downloaded file into Resonite to spawn the panel. Then open the panel's Configuration, paste your panel code (below) and press Connect.

This browser made your room code and keeps it, never in links or backups. To send from another device, type the same room code there. One device sends at a time, while its chat page is open. The panel code can only read your chat, which is public anyway. New room gives you new codes, so your panels need the new panel code. The voice keeps playing on this device.

The panel also shows your questions, pins and recap. A tap on a card there shows that viewer, from this device's data. The panel's owner can hide a viewer from it: they're added to Filters → Always hide these users here, and this page tells you.
