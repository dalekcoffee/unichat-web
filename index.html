UniChat

One window for all your stream chats. UniChat puts Twitch, TikTok, Kick, Velora, Blaze and Nimo TV chat in one place, together with alerts like subs, gifts, tips and raids.

It's made mainly for streamers keeping an eye on their own chat, and it's read-only: it never posts anything, never asks you to log in to a platform, and runs entirely in your browser.

  • Twitch, Kick, Velora, Blaze and Nimo TV only need a channel name.
  • TikTok needs a free Euler Stream API key (Settings → TikTok), because TikTok doesn't let websites read LIVE chat directly.
  • Your settings and key stay in this browser. The links in Settings open the same setup on any other device.
  • Alerts and questions collect in the Alerts panel; tick them off as you go. Click a name to see that viewer. The 🏆 Recap tab lists everyone to thank at the end of the stream.
  • Settings open in their own tab, so this page stays connected and keeps its chat while you change things.
